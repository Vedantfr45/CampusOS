WHAT WE DO

Bridging the gap
between campus & community.

CampusOS is designed to simplify campus life for everyone. Whether it's finding your next lecture hall, marking attendance seamlessly, or staying connected with your professors — we're building a unified platform that brings it all together. We believe technology should make education more accessible, not more complicated.

OUR TEAM · CRashed

Built by students,
for students.

We are Team CRashed, a group of passionate engineering students who understand the everyday challenges of campus life first-hand. Our mission is to create tools that genuinely help students navigate their academic journey and strengthen the bond between teachers and students. Every feature we build comes from real problems we've faced ourselves.
